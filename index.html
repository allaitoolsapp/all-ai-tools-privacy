Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

All AI Tools respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the All AI Tools application.

1. Information We Collect

All AI Tools does not require ordinary users to create an account or provide personal information such as name, phone number, or address to browse and use the app.

The app has a restricted Admin Panel. Administrator authentication is handled through Supabase Authentication and may involve the administrator's email address and authentication information.

2. Saved Tools

When you save an AI tool, the saved information is stored locally on your device.

Saved tools are not stored in our Supabase database.

3. Supabase

The app uses Supabase for backend functionality, including AI tools directory data and administrator authentication.

4. External Websites

The app provides links to third-party AI tools and services. When you open an AI tool's website, you are leaving the All AI Tools app and visiting a third-party website.

Third-party websites have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

All AI Tools does not intentionally collect personal information from children.
